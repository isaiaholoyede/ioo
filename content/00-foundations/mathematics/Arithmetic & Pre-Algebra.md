# Arithmetic & Pre-Algebra

## Comprehensive Arithmetic & Pre-Algebra Mastery Syllabus

#### Stage: Foundations

##### Topic: Counting, Number Sense & Place Value

- **Why it matters here:** Every later operation depends on understanding what a digit's position means and how numbers are built.
- **Resource(s):**
    1. Khan Academy — _Early Math_ course — course — free, self-paced, verified as a combined K–2 equivalent course with mastery tracking the Early math course is considered a combination of Khan Academy's Kindergarten, 1st-grade, and 2nd-grade content. [Khan Academy](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/community/posts/4407370135309-Where-does-the-course-Arithmetic-fit-in-Why-is-there-both-Early-Math-and-Grade-1)
- **Estimated time to complete:** 1–2 weeks

##### Topic: Addition & Subtraction (whole numbers)

- **Why it matters here:** The first two operations; fluency here underlies multiplication, division, and all algebraic manipulation.
- **Resource(s):**
    1. Khan Academy — _Arithmetic_ course — course — free, video + practice with mastery tracking, confirmed as an active mastery-enabled course.
- **Estimated time to complete:** 2 weeks

##### Topic: Multiplication & Division (whole numbers)

- **Why it matters here:** Required for fractions, ratios, factoring, and virtually all subsequent topics.
- **Resource(s):**
    1. Khan Academy — _Arithmetic_ course (continued) — course — same verified free platform, sequential units on multiplication/division.
- **Estimated time to complete:** 2–3 weeks

##### Topic: Order of Operations (PEMDAS)

- **Why it matters here:** Prevents ambiguity once expressions combine multiple operations — a prerequisite for every algebraic expression later.
- **Resource(s):**
    1. Khan Academy — _Arithmetic_ / Order of Operations unit — course — free, part of the same verified course sequence.
    2. MATHCOUNTS Foundation — free Practice Plan on properties/order of operations — practice set — adds competition-style problems that force correct application rather than rote recall Using the commutative, associative and distributive properties, Mathletes will arrange arithmetic problems in a different order that allows them to be solved more readily. [MATHCOUNTS Foundation](https://www.mathcounts.org/resources/free-resources-any-middle-school-math-concept)
- **Estimated time to complete:** 1 week

##### Topic: Introduction to Fractions & Basic Measurement/Time

- **Why it matters here:** First exposure to non-whole-number quantities and to real-world units, both needed before fraction arithmetic proper.
- **Resource(s):**
    1. Khan Academy — _Arithmetic_ course, fractions & measurement units — course — free, verified mastery-enabled, covers introductory fractions, time, and measurement.
- **Estimated time to complete:** 2 weeks

**Milestone Check (end of Foundations):**

- Can read, write, and compare multi-digit whole numbers by place value
- Can add, subtract, multiply, and divide multi-digit whole numbers by hand
- Can correctly apply order of operations to a multi-step expression
- Understands a fraction as "parts of a whole" and can read a basic clock/measurement scale

#### Stage: Intermediate — Core Arithmetic Fluency

##### Topic: Fraction Operations (add, subtract, multiply, divide)

- **Why it matters here:** Fractions are the most common source of arithmetic errors going forward and are foundational to ratios, algebra, and rational expressions.
- **Resource(s):**
    1. Khan Academy — _Arithmetic_ course, fractions units — course — free, mastery-tracked practice.
    2. #### Stage: Advanced — Pre-Algebra Core

##### Topic: Negative Numbers & Integer Operations

- **Why it matters here:** First formal extension of the number line beyond positive quantities; required for every algebraic operation afterward.
- **Resource(s):**

- **Why it matters here:** Compact notation for repeated multiplication, essential for algebra, geometry, and later scientific/technical work.

##### Topic: Variables, Algebraic Expressions & the "Language of Algebra"
